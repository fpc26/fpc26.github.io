## Cursory biodynamic day-types

Gardeners can observe simple day-types used with KosmoStream:

- Root days — soil work and root crops
- Leaf days — leafy greens and herbs
- Flower days — flowers and pollinator plants
- Fruit days — fruiting crops and seed

Rest periods help soil and plants between active phases. Always check current
KosmoStream Kp before planting or disturbing roots. High Kp: prefer light
surface work; defer new plantings and deep root work.

Lunar rhythms and cosmic patterns can support planting times for better soil
structure and life. Use day-types only at this cursory level.

## Simple garden practice

Focus on soil, water, compost, and regenerative habit: keep living cover,
return organic matter, water at the root zone, and build soil rather than
forcing yield. Scientific names (Latin binomials) are welcome.
