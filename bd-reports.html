Paying Online, Explained

Never paid this way before? Here's everything you need.

You don't need a bank account, a credit card on file, or to create a login. You just need a free "wallet" app — think of it like a payment app on your phone, except it holds a digital dollar called USDC. $1 of USDC is always worth exactly $1 — the same as cash, just sent over the internet instead of handed over in person.

1

Get a free wallet app

Download a wallet app on your phone or computer — Coinbase Wallet is a good place to start. It takes a few minutes to set up, just like any other app.

2

Add a few dollars

Buy a small amount of USDC right inside the app with a debit card — same as buying anything else online. Five dollars covers dozens of reports.

3

Come back and buy a report

Pick your location and date on the checkout page, choose a report, and click buy.

4

Approve the payment

Your wallet app pops up asking you to confirm a payment of a few cents. Tap approve, and your report loads immediately.

That's the whole process — nothing is stored, no account is created, and you're never charged again unless you buy another report.
