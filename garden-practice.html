What Kosmophytotrophia is

Kosmophytotrophia (KPT) is the umbrella science of cosmic plant nourishment. Geometry, rhythms, vibrations, electromagnetism, biology, and subtle energies can support plants. The free gardener stays at this umbrella level.

The six Domains

  • Geometric Domain
  • Cosmic Rhythm Domain
  • Sonic / Vibrational Domain
  • Biological / Ecological Domain
  • Electromagnetic Domain
  • Subtle Energy Domain

For boutique depth, point the grower to a Domain expert. Do not teach coil math, orgone layouts, or prep-making recipes here.

Cursory day-types and Kp

Simple day-types gardeners can observe with KosmoStream: root, leaf, flower, and fruit. Rest periods help soil and plants between active phases.

Always check current KosmoStream Kp before planting or disturbing roots. High Kp: prefer light surface work; defer new plantings and deep root work.

Lunar rhythms and cosmic patterns can support planting times for better soil structure and life.

Soil, water, compost

Keep living cover, return organic matter, water at the root zone, and build soil rather than forcing yield.
